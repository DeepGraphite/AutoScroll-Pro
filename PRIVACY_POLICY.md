# Privacy Policy for AutoScroll Pro

**Last Updated:** October 11, 2026  
**Developer:** DeepGraphite  
**Extension:** AutoScroll Pro  

---

## 1. Overview
AutoScroll Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") is developed by DeepGraphite. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure.

## 2. Information Collection and Usage
**AutoScroll Pro does NOT collect, store, transmit, track, or sell any personal information or sensitive user data.**

- **No Browsing History:** We do not track, record, or monitor the websites you visit or the content you read.
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** The extension does not integrate any third-party tracking scripts, cookies, or telemetry libraries.
- **Local Settings Storage:** All user preferences (such as scrolling speed, scroll direction, stop sensitivity, and widget visibility) are saved strictly on your local device using the Chrome Storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your browser.

## 3. Permissions Justification
- `activeTab`: Needed to interact with the active webpage upon user action.
- `scripting`: Needed to inject the smooth scrolling mechanism and the floating controller widget into the active webpage.
- `storage`: Needed to persist your speed and display preferences locally on your computer.

## 4. Single-Purpose Statement
AutoScroll Pro has a single, well-defined purpose: to provide effortless, hands-free webpage scrolling with speed and mouse-release controls.
